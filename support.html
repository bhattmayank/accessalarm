AccessAlarm Support

Need help or want to suggest an accessibility feature? Open an issue on GitHub.

Common questions

My alarm didn't ring. Open AccessAlarm and allow alarms when asked. If you said no, go to iPhone Settings → AccessAlarm and turn Alarms on.

How do I make the voice sound more natural? Download a Premium voice in Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices, then choose it in the alarm.

I am sensitive to flashing. Switch to Accessibility mode, open an alarm and turn on Gentle visuals, or leave Flash Screen and Flashlight off.

How do I delete an alarm? Swipe the alarm left on the main screen and choose Delete.
