AccessAlarm

Alarms designed for accessibility, for autistic and sensitive people, and kid-friendly too.

Big buttons, calm visuals and your choice of how an alarm reaches you: sound, spoken message, flashing screen, flashlight, vibration or a friendly animated character. Set a start time, an end time and how often it repeats, and see exactly when it will ring.

Made for

  • Deaf and hard of hearing: flashing screen in a colour you choose, flashlight and vibration.
  • Blind and low vision: spoken alarms that say the time and your message, VoiceOver support, high contrast, black and white and extra-large text.
  • Autistic and sensory-sensitive people: Gentle visuals (slow fade, no flashing), no surprises, and every option can be turned off.
  • Kids: a playful Kids mode with large buttons and animated friends. Adults get a clean, professional mode.

Private by design

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your device. Read the privacy policy.
